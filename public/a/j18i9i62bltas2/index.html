Digital Marketing and Website Services Agreement

Kind Supply Co LLC · d/b/a Kind Supply Digital

THIS DIGITAL MARKETING AND WEBSITE SERVICES AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”) is entered into as of the Effective Date (as defined in Section 1.1) by and between KIND SUPPLY CO LLC, a Virginia limited liability company doing business as Kind Supply Digital (“Provider”), and the business identified by the business name, customer name, and email address provided at checkout (“Client”). Provider and Client are sometimes referred to herein individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Provider is engaged in the business of website design and development, search engine optimization, and digital advertising management;

WHEREAS, Client owns and operates a dental practice at two (2) office locations; and

WHEREAS, Client desires to engage Provider to perform the services described herein for one (1) of its office locations, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement, with services for its second office location to be addressed, if at all, by a separate written agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 Effective Date. “Effective Date” means the date on which Client completes the first payment through Provider’s online checkout as provided in Section 5.

1.2 Covered Location. “Covered Location” means the one (1) dental office of Client designated by Client at or following checkout and confirmed by Provider in writing, which confirmation is incorporated into this Agreement.

1.3 Additional Location. “Additional Location” means any office owned or operated by Client or its owners or affiliates other than the Covered Location, now or in the future, including without limitation Client’s second existing office.

1.4 Initial Term. “Initial Term” means the period of six (6) consecutive months commencing on the Effective Date.

1.5 Monthly Fee. “Monthly Fee” means Two Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($2,000.00).

1.6 Term Commitment. “Term Commitment” means the aggregate of six (6) Monthly Fees, totaling Twelve Thousand and No/100 Dollars ($12,000.00).

1.7 Client Materials. “Client Materials” means Client’s logos, trademarks, photographs, text, business information, data, and domain name.

1.8 Provider Materials. “Provider Materials” means the website’s source code, design, layouts, templates, and hosting environment, together with Provider’s tools, systems, methods, and know-how.

1.9 Ad Spend. “Ad Spend” means all amounts charged by Google or any other advertising platform for the placement of advertisements.

2. Engagement and Scope of Services

2.1 Engagement. Client hereby engages Provider, and Provider agrees, to perform the following services for the Covered Location only (collectively, the “Services”):

(a) Website Development. Design, development, and launch of a custom, mobile-responsive website of up to twenty (20) pages, including home, about, team, contact, and individual service pages, with appointment-request or online-booking integration, analytics and conversion tracking, and two (2) rounds of design revisions. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to launch the website within thirty (30) days after receipt of all Client Materials and approvals required under Section 7;

(b) Search Engine Optimization. Ongoing technical, on-page, and local search engine optimization, including service-page optimization, Google Business Profile optimization, local citation management, and indexing management;

(c) Google Ads Management. Account audit and configuration, keyword research, negative-keyword management, ad group structure aligned with the website’s service pages, ad copy, conversion tracking, and ongoing bid, budget, and performance optimization within the budget approved by Client;

(d) Hosting and Maintenance. Hosting of the website on Provider’s infrastructure, security and platform updates, and minor edits not to exceed five (5) hours per month; and

(e) Reporting. A monthly written report of website traffic, search visibility, and Google Ads performance.

2.2 Excluded Services. The Services do not include e-commerce functionality, patient portals, custom software, translation, website pages in excess of twenty (20), or any service for the Additional Location.

2.3 Changes in Scope. Any service not described in Section 2.1 shall be performed only pursuant to a written change order signed by both Parties stating the additional work and its price.

2.4 Manner of Performance. Provider shall determine the methods, personnel, subcontractors, and software tools used to perform the Services and shall remain responsible for their performance hereunder.

3. Term

3.1 Initial Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for the Initial Term. Client’s obligation to pay the full Term Commitment is fixed and unconditional, except as provided in Section 12.2.

3.2 Continuation. Unless either Party delivers written notice of non-continuation at least thirty (30) days before the expiration of the Initial Term, the search engine optimization and Google Ads management Services shall continue on a month-to-month basis at the Monthly Fee, terminable by either Party upon thirty (30) days’ written notice. If Client discontinues such Services but elects to keep the website hosted by Provider, Client shall pay Ninety-Nine and No/100 Dollars ($99.00) per month for hosting and maintenance. If Client elects neither, Section 8.4 or Section 12.3 shall apply.

4. Compensation and Payment

4.1 Fees. Client shall pay Provider the Monthly Fee for each month of the Initial Term. The first Monthly Fee is due on the Effective Date, and each subsequent Monthly Fee is due on the same calendar day of each succeeding month (or the last day of any month having no such day).

4.2 Website Included Under License. No separate fee is charged for website development. The website is developed and provided to Client under license as part of the Services, in consideration of Client’s Term Commitment. Ownership of the website is not included in the Monthly Fee and may be acquired only as provided in Section 8.4.

4.3 Automatic Payment. Each Monthly Fee, and any other amount due hereunder, shall be charged to the payment method Client provides at checkout on the date such amount becomes due, without further invoice or approval, as authorized in Section 5.5.

4.4 Late Payment. Any amount not received within five (5) days after its due date shall incur a one-time late charge equal to ten percent (10%) of the overdue amount. Client shall pay a fee of Thirty-Five and No/100 Dollars ($35.00) for any declined or returned payment and shall furnish a valid replacement payment method within three (3) business days.

4.5 Billing Disputes. Client shall notify Provider in writing of any disputed charge within ten (10) days after the charge, stating the amount disputed and the basis for the dispute. Client shall timely pay all undisputed amounts. Any charge not so disputed shall be deemed accepted.

4.6 No Setoff. Client shall not withhold, set off, or reduce any amount due hereunder on account of any claim, dispute, or dissatisfaction with the results of the Services.

4.7 Fees Earned. Each Monthly Fee is fully earned when due in consideration of Provider’s reservation of capacity and performance of the Services, and is non-refundable except as required by law.

4.8 Taxes. All fees are exclusive of sales, use, and similar taxes, which shall be borne by Client.

6.1 Exclusion. This Agreement applies solely to the Covered Location. No Services shall be provided for the Additional Location hereunder.
